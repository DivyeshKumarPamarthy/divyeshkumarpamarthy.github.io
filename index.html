    01 Flagship · AI platform

    SmartHire AI

    AI-driven interview & candidate evaluation platform

    Candidates upload a résumé, get role-specific questions, answer out loud to a live voice interviewer, and receive a scored, itemised report. Recruiters compare candidates on evidence instead of gut feel.

    Problem
    Candidates rarely get objective, itemised feedback before a real interview, and most screening tools are built for the employer.
    My role
    Lead developer during the Infosys Springboard internship. The program mentor set up the repository; I wrote the full-stack application (modules 4 to 10) and the Docker / Render deployment, 20 of the repo's 28 commits.
    Key decision
    The recording is confirmed before transcription starts and scoring runs as a background task, so a model outage never costs a candidate their answer. Engagement is measured in the browser and kept out of the score.
    Outcome
    A working three-role platform whose dashboards only show numbers traceable to real data, covered by 490 automated test cases.

    What I built

    • FastAPI + PostgreSQL backend with JWT and Google OAuth2 sign-in, plus role-based access for Candidate, Recruiter and Admin.
    • Parses uploaded résumés and generates role-specific questions through a pluggable AI layer: Google Gemini or local Ollama models.
    • WebSocket voice interviewer with speech analysis, and MediaPipe face-landmark tracking in the browser for engagement and integrity signals.
    • Automated answer scoring, shortlist insights and downloadable PDF reports, in React dashboards with live analytics and per-endpoint latency metrics.

    02 RAG · ML

    Syllabus Note AI

    AI-powered study notes, scoped to your syllabus

    Upload a textbook PDF and your syllabus topics. Instead of summarising the whole book, it retrieves only what the syllabus asks for and turns it into structured, exam-ready notes.

    Problem
    General AI tools summarise an entire textbook; students need notes on exactly the topics their syllabus lists.
    My role
    Solo project: ML pipeline, backend, frontend and product design.
    Key decision
    A hybrid ML + RAG pipeline: a fine-tuned DistilBERT classifier drops noisy chunks before retrieved text reaches the LLM, so notes stay on topic.
    Outcome
    A complete SaaS flow: sign up, upload a PDF, choose topics, and get notes saved to your history and exportable as PDF.

    What I built

    • Retrieval-augmented generation over a FAISS vector store with HuggingFace sentence-transformer embeddings, for accurate, context-aware retrieval.
    • A fine-tuned DistilBERT classifier filters out textbook noise, such as tables of contents and stray diagrams, before anything reaches the LLM.
    • FastAPI backend with JWT authentication, SQLAlchemy ORM and fully documented REST APIs. Every generated note is saved to the user's history.
    • Runs locally on Ollama or on OpenAI models; a Next.js frontend exports notes to PDF in one click.

    03 Web · async workflows

    Trekking Management App

    Multi-role web & async workflow platform

    One platform for administrators, trek staff and trekkers: booking, slot management and approvals, with the slow work moved off the request path.

    Problem
    Treks involve several roles, limited slots and approvals, and sending notifications or building reports inside web requests makes an app slow.
    My role
    Built the platform: REST APIs, background workers and the role dashboards.
    Key decision
    Notifications and report generation run on asynchronous Redis + Celery workers instead of blocking requests.

    What I built

    • Multi-role platform (Administrator, Trek Staff, Trekker) with authentication, trek booking, slot management and approval workflows.
    • RESTful APIs in Flask, with notifications and automated report generation offloaded to asynchronous Redis + Celery workers.
    • Role-specific dashboards with real-time analytics.

    04 Full-stack · CRUD

    Placement Portal

    Full-stack placement management system

    Runs campus recruitment end to end: companies post placement drives, students apply, and admins keep the whole process honest.

    Problem
    Campus recruitment juggles companies, students and drives, each with approvals, eligibility rules and deadlines.
    My role
    Solo build, submitted as an IIT Madras BS project.
    Key decision
    An admin approval gate: companies and drives must be approved, and accounts can be deactivated, before anything reaches students.

    What I built

    • Three roles with access control: admins approve companies and drives and can deactivate accounts; companies publish and manage drives; students apply and track results.
    • Full CRUD for drives, profiles and applications, with an application status flow from Applied to Shortlisted to Selected or Rejected.
    • Responsive UI for application tracking and result management.
